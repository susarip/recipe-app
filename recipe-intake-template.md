# Recipe intake template

For recipes that only exist in a video, a story, or someone's head. Copy the block below once per
recipe, fill in what you can, and leave anything you don't know blank. Write it the way the creator
said it; don't translate to the app's fields. I do the structuring (canonical names, swaps, make-ahead
windows) and flag anything I had to guess in the recipe's review notes.

Rules of thumb:
- Ingredients: one per line, with the amount the way it was said ("a handful", "2 scoops", "half a block").
  If no amount was given, write the ingredient alone.
- Steps: one per line, in order, in your own words. Include times and temperatures if they were said.
- Say what the video showed but didn't say, when it matters ("he used frozen peppers", "it was a 12 inch pan").
- Anything about leftovers, freezing, meal prep, or "this keeps all week" goes under Notes.
