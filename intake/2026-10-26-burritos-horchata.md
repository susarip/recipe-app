## Recipe 1

Title: chicken and cheese burrito
Source :
Makes (4 servings):
Time: idk

Ingredients:
-1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
-1 packet taco seasoning (i have taco seasoning at home, this should in tsps or tbsp so i can use my pantry ingredients)
-200g 2% greek yogurt
-80 g tomato sauce/crushed tomatoes
-2 laughing cow wedges 
-2 tbsp cilantro
-8-10 tbsp of veggies (recipe used 3 tbs corn, 2 tbsp black beans, 3 tbsl pico de gallo)
-50 g of cheese of choice (he used pepper jack but low moisture mozz, cheddar, and similar would work i think)
-tortillas

Steps:
1. season chicken with half of taco seasoning, cook in oven, pan or air fryer
2. mix everything together else except remaining taco seasoning and cheese into pan and melt together
3. add in taco seasoning
4. add cooked chopped chicken into pan and mix 
4. turn off heat and add in cheese
5. let mixture cool in fridge for 30-40 minutes
6. put in tortilla and roll, wrap in foil, and freeze for later
7. to eat, heat on a pan or in the air fryer or over

## Recipe 2

Title: horchata
Source:
Makes: idk
Time: 15 minutes active, 24 hours passive

Ingredients:
- 1 cup white rice
- 6 cups water
- cinnamon sticks
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk
- tbsp vanilla
- 1 cup white sugar
- 2 cups whole milk

Steps:
1. soak rice and cinnamon sticks in 4 cups water for a day
2. add everything to blender (not the last 2 cups of water)
3. blend and strain
4. add 2 cups water and serve over ice
